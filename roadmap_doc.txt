Category 1: Telephony Lifecycle, Background Execution & OS Crash Loops (P1 Blockers)
[X] INC-01: ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException on Main Thread
● Component: HardenedTelephonyObserverService / WatchdogSafeBootManager
● Trigger: Android 12+ (API 31+) / Android 14 (API 34) background execution restrictions (mAllowStartForeground = false).
● Stack Trace / Error:
●
● text
●   Uncaught Exception in main: Unable to create service com.cellular.rpc.transport.service.HardenedTelephonyObserverService: android.app.ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException: Service.startForeground() not allowed due to mAllowStartForeground false: service com.aistudio.cellularrpc.qxmpz/com.cellular.rpc.transport.service.HardenedTelephonyObserverService
● Failure Analysis: The app invokes startForegroundService() when the app process lacks an active foreground window or qualifying foreground service exemption. On modern Android versions, calling Service.startForeground() without an active UI window or high-priority FCM notification immediately throws a fatal exception directly on the main thread, terminating the process.
● Watchdog Crash Loop Escalation: WatchdogSafeBootManager intercepts the process crash, flags it as a failure, initiates a safe-boot restart, and attempts to spin up HardenedTelephonyObserverService again while still in the background. This creates an unrecoverable crash loop that drains battery and causes the OS to put the app into restricted app standby.
● Resolution Spec: Completely decouple telephony ingestion from HardenedTelephonyObserverService. Demote or remove the foreground service entirely. In WatchdogSafeBootManager, implement exponential backoff and a hard retry ceiling (max 2 attempts) before halting restart loops.
[X] INC-02: 30-Minute Telephony Ingestion Blackout (6:18 PM – 6:48 PM EDT)
● Component: Inbound Telephony Pipeline / SQLite Message Store
● Observed Behavior: No incoming messages received or processed between 6:18 PM and 6:48 PM EDT. Messages sent during this window only arrived in a delayed burst after the app was manually brought back into the foreground at 6:48 PM.
● Root Cause: Directly correlated with the ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException crash at 6:18 PM. When the service crashed and the process died, in-memory ContentObserver instances (content://mms-sms and content://sms/inbox) were destroyed. Inbound cellular PDUs remained buffered at the carrier/modem layer or in the system telephony provider until manual activity resurrection triggered a database scan.
● Resolution Spec: Migrate from dynamic in-memory observers to static manifest-registered BroadcastReceiver components (SMS_RECEIVED, DATA_SMS_RECEIVED) using goAsync(). goAsync() provides an asynchronous execution window of up to 30 seconds on a background thread without requiring a foreground service or persistent notification.
INC-03: Architectural Rejection of Default SMS Handler Role
● Component: Telephony Architecture & System Permissions
● Constraint: AIUI is explicitly prohibited from becoming or requesting to be the system Default SMS App.
● Implications:
1. The app will never receive android.provider.Telephony.SMS_DELIVER_ACTION or WAP_PUSH_DELIVER_ACTION (which are reserved exclusively for the default SMS app).
1. The app must rely on android.provider.Telephony.SMS_RECEIVED and WAP_PUSH_RECEIVED_ACTION.
1. Because it is a secondary companion app, Android does not grant automatic background execution exemptions. Telephony receivers must handle payloads immediately within onReceive() / goAsync(), write directly to AIUI's Room database, and enqueue long-running work (e.g. MMS network downloads) via WorkManager.

Category 2: Carrier Telephony, APN Transport & Shadowban Failover
INC-04: Carrier Concatenated SMS Throttling & Silent Drop Ceiling (256B–280B)
● Component: Outbound Cellular Transport / MT Carrier Firewall
● Observed Behavior: Plain SMS payloads ≤256 bytes (probes 1 through 4) deliver reliably with CRC verification. Plain SMS payloads ≥280 bytes (probes 5A, 5B, 5C up to 512B) are silently dropped or quarantined by the TracFone/Verizon carrier network without delivering to the handset or returning an SMSC error.
● Carrier Filtering Mechanics: The carrier spam/velocity firewall triggers an automated shadowban cooldown when concatenated multi-segment SMS (>256B / 3+ segments) are detected from the gateway. Handset-to-AI (MO) messages remain unthrottled, while AI-to-Handset (MT) messages are silently suppressed for 15–30 minutes.
● Resolution Spec: AI Egress transport policy must enforce strict payload tiering:
  ○ Tier 0: Single-part plain SMS strictly capped at ≤250 bytes.
  ○ Tier 1: Payloads >250 bytes or containing formatted data/blueprints must be automatically promoted to an MMS bundle.
INC-05: MMS Zero-Rated APN Transport & 32x32 Logo Attachment Requirement
● Component: MMS Bearer / Zero-Rated Carrier APN
● Observed Behavior: MMS messages deliver out-of-band even when mobile data is toggled off and Wi-Fi is disconnected, using the carrier's whitelisted MMS APN (vzwinternet/mms). However:
1. Sending standalone text via MMS often results in the carrier MMSC stripping the text body or dropping the message entirely.
1. Bundling a tiny binary media attachment (a 32x32 PNG AIUI logo) forces the MMSC to route the payload through the media gateway, ensuring 100% delivery.
1. Text payloads must be embedded directly inside the MMS smil caption / subject field rather than a detached text part.
● MMS Caption Payload Envelope Ceiling: Empirically verified through sizing ladder tests:
  ○ 600B, 700B, 800B, 1000B, 1229B, 1431B, 1591B, 2032B, 2207B, 2832B, and 2868B wire sizes delivered and verified with CRC.
  ○ Payloads ≥3000B (Probe 10) are dropped or truncated by the carrier MMSC.
  ○ Hard upper ceiling for uncompressed MMS caption payloads is established at 2,950 bytes.
INC-06: Telephony Ingestion Race Condition on MMS Delivery
● Component: MmsContentObserver / Cursor Watermark Tracker
● Observed Behavior: MMS Probe 15 (~2990B) delivered successfully to the handset and was visible in Google Messages, but completely failed to appear in AIUI's chat thread.
● Root Cause: Race condition in cursor polling or watermark tracking within HardenedTelephonyObserverService. When an MMS arrives, the system writes the notification PDU first, then downloads the binary parts asynchronously. The observer polled content://mms before parts were fully written to telephony storage, updated its internal timestamp watermark, and never re-scanned the completed record.
● Resolution Spec: Replace table cursor polling with a dedicated BroadcastReceiver listening for WAP_PUSH_RECEIVED_ACTION with MIME type application/vnd.wap.mms-message. Enqueue download and ingestion via WorkManager with input data containing the PDU URI to guarantee atomic parsing once parts are assembled.

Category 3: Client Message Parsing, Command Interception & UI Bleeding
INC-07: Unintended Local Command Interception & Telephony Bypass
● Component: OfflineCommandRouter / Intent Classifier
● Observed Behavior: When typing conversational messages referencing "offline mode", "v27", or UI updates, the internal router falsely intercepted the text as an offline command, generated a mock response ("Pally AI: Local command acknowledged. UI has been updated (Offline Mode)"), marked it "0B SMS • CRC Verified", and never placed any PDU onto the cellular network.
● Root Cause: Natural-language regex and fuzzy keyword matching running directly on the outgoing text field without an explicit command delimiter.
● Resolution Spec: Enforce a strict slash-command syntax boundary:
  ○ Only strings beginning with a leading / (e.g., /theme, /widget, /safe, /net) are eligible for client-side local interception.
  ○ All other text—regardless of keywords—must be treated strictly as conversational data and dispatched over cellular transport.
INC-08: Premature UI Hydration & Simulation Mode Message Flood
● Component: ChatViewModel / Initial State Hydration / Telephony Sync
● Observed Behavior:
1. Before runtime telephony permissions are granted, the chat screen hydrates with mock conversation bubbles and an active weather card stub.
1. The moment runtime SMS permissions are granted, the app immediately scans the device's system SMS inbox and dumps the full unpaginated message history into the chat feed while simulation/loopback mode is active.
● Resolution Spec:
  ○ Keep the UI in an uninitialized clean state until onboarding and permission gates are cleared.
  ○ Completely disable historical system SMS sync while simulation/loopback mode is active.
  ○ When syncing live device SMS, implement strict pagination (limit initial query to latest 25 messages) and sanitize historical raw code/widget payloads to plain text so they do not overwhelm Compose recomposition.

INC-09: WidgetModels.parse Key Mismatches
● Component: WidgetModels.parse
● Observed Behavior:
  ○ Weather Widget: Failed to bind temperature (0°F) and location (Unknown) when passed temperature and location. Resolved only when payload supplied temp and city.

INC-10: Market Ticker In-Place State Reconciliation Failure
● Component: MarketTickerCard Composable / State Hoisting
● Observed Behavior: When a new market_ticker payload is received for an existing card, the rendered card does not mutate its state in place.
● Root Cause: Missing widget instance identity (widget_id) in Compose state management. Compose treats each inbound message as an appended immutable item in LazyColumn rather than reconciling an existing card state by unique ID.
● Resolution Spec: Hoist widget state into a keyed repository (Map). When an incoming payload arrives with a matching widget_id, mutate the existing state object to trigger localized Compose recomposition without scrolling or re-rendering the feed.
INC-11: Market Ticker Inline Search & Loading Shimmer UX Gap

1. On Enter, immediately transition the card into an animated shimmer/pulse loading state.
1. Dispatch an outbound cellular request ({"type":"market_ticker","symbol":"$INPUT"}).
1. Hydrate the card and clear loading state when the replacement payload returns.
INC-12: Client JSON Parsing Faults & Lack of Self-Healing Loop
● Component: WidgetModels.parse / Telemetry Recovery
● Error Example: JSON Parsing Fault: Expected ':' after ... at character 22 of {"type":"weather",...}
● Observed Behavior: A malformed token or syntax character in an incoming cellular payload throws an unhandled exception, causing the widget to abort hydration and remain stuck as an empty fallback stub.
● Resolution Spec:
1. Integrate FuzzyStreamDemuxer (commit ea6c33a) directly into the cellular ingestion pipeline to repair truncated or malformed JSON streams prior to deserialization.
1. Implement a client-side telemetry ping: if deserialization fails, emit an automated diagnostic resync frame ({"action":"resync","widget_type":"...","widget_id":"..."}) to request a clean replacement payload from the gateway.

6.1 Telephony & Ingestion Defects
* [INC-20260911-05] Voice Note (Audio MMS) Missing from In-App Thread
   * Observed Behavior: Inbound voice note arrived cleanly in Android's default messaging app (Google Messages), but did not appear in the AIUI conversation feed.

   * Observed Behavior: Switching away from the Chat tab and returning causes the entire message thread to visibly re-render and scroll down from the top item to the latest message.

[INC-07] Outbound MMS Promotion Triggers Android App Chooser & Exposes Anchor Logo
* Severity / Priority: P2 (Functional / UX Leak)
* Component: OutboundMmsDispatcher / EgressTierManager
* Trigger: Composing and sending a multi-PDU (6 PDU) message in the AIUI chat composer.
* Observed Behavior:
1. Tapping send inside AIUI did not transmit in the background; instead, it launched the Android system intent chooser (Intent.createChooser or implicit ACTION_SEND / ACTION_SENDTO).
2. Selecting Google Messages populated the draft with the 6-PDU text body alongside the internal 32x32 visual anchor (bluish square with blue circle inside).
3. User had to manually delete the image attachment inside Google Messages to send plain concatenated SMS.
* Root Cause Analysis:
   * Because AIUI runs as a non-default companion app, direct background MMS transmission (SmsManager.sendMultimediaMessage) requires either carrier APN access or elevated telephony carrier permissions. If sendMultimediaMessage is unconfigured or hits an exception, the fallback path delegates to Intent.ACTION_SEND (image/* + text) rather than silently handling cellular radio queuing.
   * The internal 32x32 visual anchor (required for carrier zero-rated APN routing) was exposed in the user-visible intent bundle.
* Required AI Studio Fix:
1. Direct MMS Dispatch: Ensure SmsManager.sendMultimediaMessage() or carrier APN socket is attempted directly without delegating to external UI intents.
2. Fallback Guard: If direct dispatch fails or requires system delegation, do not dump the raw media anchor into user-facing intent pickers without a clear in-app dispatch state.


Deep Dive: Outbound Multi-PDU MMS Promotion & Google Messages Intent Fallback
* Root Cause: When the outgoing message exceeded 256 bytes (6 PDUs), EgressTierManager promoted it from plain SMS to MMS to avoid carrier multi-part text drops. However, instead of dispatching the MMS PDU directly via SmsManager.sendMultimediaMessage() with a content URI to a generated PDU buffer, the client fell back to an Android system ACTION_SEND intent with type = "image/png" and the message text as EXTRA_TEXT.

1. Persisted In-Flight Counter Deadlock: SlidingWindowFlowController tracks in-flight packets (e.g. WINDOW: 68/4 IN-FLIGHT). AIUI
{

9.3 Client-Side Ingestion Pipeline
1. Markdown Stream Interception: ChatFeedScreen and the message parser treat ``aiui as a first-class block token. Conversational text renders as standard chat bubbles, and theaiui` block is completely stripped from prose.
2. Typed Payload Dispatcher:

* type: "sdui": Deserialized into WidgetBlueprint and inflated inline via DynamicAppHost.

1. Fuzzy Stream & Chunk Healing: Explicit boundary fences allow FuzzyStreamDemuxer to reliably detect chunk boundaries across multi-part SMS/MMS segments, preventing parser crashes on partial chunks ([INC-12]).
